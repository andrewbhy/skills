# Design feedback vocabulary

Use a word with a target and a constraint: “quieter, inspector metadata;
preserve selected-row emphasis.” Before a job starts, the coordinator restates
the scoped change, affected targets and what must stay intact. Resolve ambiguity
before dependent work; no slash commands or installation are needed.

| Word | Meaning | What changes | What must not change |
|---|---|---|---|
| bolder | Strengthen hierarchy or a defining idea. | Emphasis, contrast, scale or signature treatment on named targets. | Readability, density, task order, approved identity; no automatic decoration. |
| quieter | Reduce competing stimulation. | Secondary emphasis, chrome weight, competing colour or motion. | Clear selection, focus, errors and primary actions. |
| polish | Finish local inconsistencies. | Alignment, spacing, state consistency and small defects. | Concept, behaviour and scope; redesign needs its own decision. |
| typeset | Improve text hierarchy and reading. | Roles, weight, measure, leading and wrapping. | Meaning, terminology, desktop legibility and approved font choices. |
| layout | Improve composition and grouping. | Placement, proximity, spacing rhythm and allocation. | Task order, reachable actions, functional geometry and stable anchors. |
| colorize | Give colour a clearer job. | Approved token roles, accent distribution and surface contrast. | Approved palette, contrast, selection/focus distinction and state semantics. |
| delight | Add a purposeful memorable detail. | One named signature moment or feedback detail, within the motion gate. | Task speed, reduced motion, readability; no ambient attention demand. |
| simplify / distill | Remove redundancy and competing structure. | Extra frames, duplicated labels and unnecessary choices. | Information, discoverability, essential controls and lasting state facts. |
| clarify | Make meaning easier to understand. | Labels, instructions, errors and information hierarchy. | Facts, product terminology and behaviour. |
| harden | Make the design withstand real use. | Long/non-Latin text, volume, empty/error/loading states and interaction edges. | Intended concept, normal-case quality, focus, drafts and user data. |
| critique (review) | Judge fit and specificity. | Ranked observations and proposed scoped fixes, grounded in references. | Files and behaviour; review alone authorizes no edits or approval. |
| audit (evidence) | Measure and document technical issues. | Reproducible evidence, targets, severity and coverage gaps. | Files and behaviour; distinguish verified failures from heuristic warnings and unknowns. |

A word never overrides a recorded Keep or Strong objection. If directions
conflict, surface that conflict rather than silently widening the job. Critique
starts with independent design judgment; a clean audit does not establish good
design. Use the [motion vocabulary](motion-vocabulary.md) for exact effect names
and the [brief](design-brief.md) to record decisions. Adapted in jig's words from
Impeccable; [source and licence](README.md#borrowed-practices-and-attribution).
